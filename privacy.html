Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-06

What data is accessed

With the operator's consent, the app uses Google Drive and Google Sheets scopes to read brief drafts from the operator's spreadsheet, upload generated PDF and JSON files to the operator's Drive folders, and write run logs to the operator's spreadsheet.

How data is used and shared

Data is used only to produce and store the operator's own daily briefs. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No data from other Google users is accessed.

Storage and deletion

Files stay in the operator's Google Drive and Google Sheets. Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions.
